Agentic House Search Data Registry

Data registry Postcode report

Publicly available UK open datasets and machine-readable APIs for agentic house and neighbourhood search — crime, demographics, affluence, property prices, noise, transport, amenities, schools and environment. Each entry lists the dataset home, the API endpoint, licence, update cadence, and the questions the data can answer. Compiled 2 Aug 2026.

Sources are © their respective publishers — ONS, Home Office/Police, HM Land Registry, Defra, Environment Agency, DfT, TfL, DfE, Ofsted, FSA, NHS England, Ofcom, GLA and OpenStreetMap contributors — mostly under the Open Government Licence v3. "Extras" entries are adjacent open datasets commonly used alongside these.
